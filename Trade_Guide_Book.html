Part 1 — System ka saar (2 minute me samjho)

Kya karte ho: Har trading din 15:45 IST pe dashboard NSE 500 ka scan karke "STRICT TOP-3" stocks deta hai jo fresh GTF demand zone pe baithe hain. Aap agle din limit order se zone me entry lete ho, strict SL ke saath, aur max 5 din (1 week) me T1/T2 target nikalte ho. Yeh 1-week swing trading hai.
Backtest ke honest numbers (2.5 saal, 122 filled trades):

Win rate~42%10 trades me ~4 jeetenge — 6 me SL hit hoga aur ye NORMAL hai
Avg per trade+0.31RJeet 2R deti hai, haar sirf 1R leti hai → is balance se profit

Is system ka fayda SL discipline aur R-multiples me hai, stock-picking magic me nahi. Jo is routine ko 100% follow karega, uske numbers is table jaisa rahenge. Jo rules todega, uska alag hisaab hoga.

📅 Din-wise special notes

DinKya khaas hai
MONDAY• Friday ki picks ke orders aaj active honge (agar Friday sham place kiye the).
• Weekend gap check: agar stock SL ke neeche open ho → aaj entry mat lo, order cancel. (Gap-up open above zone ho to bhi chase mat karo — limit order apne aap fill hoga warna skip.)
• 15:45 pe Monday ki fresh picks aayengi (Tuesday entry ke liye).
TUESDAY• Monday picks ka entry window (Tue–Thu, 3 din).
• Purane unfilled orders ka count yaad rakho — 3 din poore = cancel.
WEDNESDAY• Mid-week = max activity: naye fills + purane positions ka monitor.
• Time-stop check: jo position pichhle Wednesday enter hui thi, aaj uska 5th din hai → SL/T1/T2 nahi laga to aaj close pe exit (TIME).
THURSDAY• Weekly options expiry — index me noise ho sakta hai, stocks me koi rule change nahi.
• Monday picks ka aakhri entry din — sham tak fill nahi to cancel.
FRIDAY• 15:45 ki picks Monday ke liye hongi — weekend gap risk. Optional rule: comfort nahi hai to Friday picks skip karo (signals kam, neend achi).
• Time-stop: Monday entry wali positions ka 5th din aaj → close pe exit agar SL/T1/T2 nahi laga.
• Weekend me journal complete karo, Saturday review ke liye data ready rakho.
Entry window ka rule (bahut zaroori): Pick ke agle din se 3 trading din tak entry zone ka wait karo. 3 din me fill nahi hua → order cancel, trade skip (backtest me ~34% orders aise skip hote hain — normal). Chase karna = SL-hit badhana. Jo stock bhaag gaya, gaya — agle din naya scan aayega.

Part 4 — Trade kaise lena hai (entry → exit, live example)

Real example — dashboard ka actual strict pick M&MFIN:

Dashboard ne bataya:
Entry zoneSLT1T2Risk/share
₹387.83 – ₹388.80₹385.69₹395.02₹398.13₹3.11
Step 1 — Qty nikalo: Risk ₹1,000 ÷ ₹3.11 = 321 shares
Step 2 — Orders lagao:
  • Limit Buy: 321 sh @ ₹388.80 (zone ka upar wala edge — entry range me fill hoga)
  • SL-M: trigger ₹385.70, market sell 321 sh (GTT me dono ek saath set hota hai)
  • Targets: GTT sell 160 sh @ ₹395.02 + GTT sell 161 sh @ ₹398.13 (ya manually manage karo)
Step 3 — Manage:
  • T1 touch → 160 sh sell (+₹996 profit), baaki 161 sh ka SL ₹388.80 (cost) pe le jao
  • T2 touch → 161 sh sell (+₹1,503 profit) → total ≈ +₹2,500 (2.5R) ✅
  • SL touch → -₹998 (-1R) — accept karo, journal likho, aage badho
  • 5 din me kuch nahi → close pe exit (TIME), average -0.2R — bhi accept karo
Step 4 — Result journal me likho (Profit Table me row bharo).
Exit Rules — ek line me:
  1. SL pehle — SL touch hua to trade khatam, koi bahana nahi.
  2. T1 pe aadha — 50% position book, baaki ka SL cost pe (ab ye trade FREE hai).
  3. T2 pe baaki — 50% book.
  4. 5-din time stop — SL/T1/T2 nahi laga to 5th din close pe exit, jo bhi price ho.
  5. 3-din entry stop — fill nahi hui to cancel.

Order placement ka exact flow (har sham):
1. Qty calculate karo (Risk ÷ risk-per-share).
2. Buy Limit @ entryHi (zone ka top) — raat me AMO/GTT se.
3. Sell SL-M @ SL se thoda neeche trigger (broker ke hisaab se).
4. Targets ya to GTT se rakho, ya din me manually manage karo (recommended — T1 ke baad SL adjust karna hai).
5. Morning 9:15 pe verify: order active hai? Price SL ke neeche open to nahi hua?
6. Open < SL → entry order cancel. Open > zone → limit apne aap fill hoga jab price wapas aayega (3 din window).

🚦 CIRCUIT BREAKER (capital bachane ka niyam):
  • Max 2 open positions ek saath (2R risk se zyada kabhi nahi).
  • Lagataar 3 SL → 2 trading din poora band. (Market mood badal gaya — system us waqt match nahi kar raha.)
  • Lagataar 4 SL → poori week band, Saturday review me reason dhundho.
  • Mahina -5R pe → agle mahine risk aadha (0.5%) kar do. Mahina +5R pe wapas 1%.
  • Position sizing kabhi 1% se upar mat badhao — chaahe kitna bhi confident feel ho. Backtest me -6.6R wale months aate hain; 2% risk pe wo -13% capital hota.

Part 8 — Psychology: 10 Golden Rules

🧠 DISCIPLINE RULES (ye system ka asli engine hai)

  1. NO PICKS = WAIT. Khali din normal hai (~2 picks/week average). Bina signal trade karna = guaranteed loss ka raasta.
  2. Sirf dashboard ke STRICT picks. Man se RELIANCE lena, news se TATASTEEL lena — sab banned. Dashboard ne 2.5 saal ka data dekhke filter banaya hai, aapne nahi.
  3. SL kabhi wide mat karo. "Thoda neeche rakh deta hoon" wale ne hi -81R drawdown banaya tha. SL = system ka engine.
  4. Chase mat karo. Stock zone chhod ke upar chala gaya? Skip. Limit order hi entry hai. (IN-zone chase: backtest me win 19%.)
  5. SL ke baad revenge mat lo. -1R = deal ka hissa. Journal likho, close karo, agle din ka scan dekho.
  6. P&L din me 20 baar mat dekho. Orders lage hain, rules set hain. Screen staring se SL nahi rukta, sirf dimaag kharab hota hai.
  7. Har trade SAME din likho journal me — nhi to 2 hafte baad aapko pata hi nahi chalega kya galat hua.
  8. Expectation sahi rakho: +0.31R avg, 42% win — yaani roz ka paisa nahi, mahine ka paisa. Get-rich nahi, steady edge.
  9. Dusre se compare mat karo. Aapke numbers backtest baseline se compare hone chahiye (Part 11), Telegram group se nahi.
  10. System pe bharosa = rules pe bharosa. Agar aapne 20 trades ke baad bhi rules nahi tode aur loss me ho — tab system review karo. Rules todke loss = system ki galti nahi, aapki.

  4. Agle week ka plan likho — 1 line: "Ise week X improve karunga." (Sirf 1 cheez, 10 nahi.)

Part 11 — Monthly review & system upgrade

Har mahine ke aakhri Saturday — 1 ghanta:
  1. Mahine ka total R nikalo. Baseline: +2.5R se +3.5R average (10 trades × 0.31R).
  2. 20-30 trades complete hone ke baad hi koi conclusion lo — 10 trades me kuch bhi ho sakta hai.
  3. Agar 30 trades me avgR < 0 aur aapne rules nahi tode → mujhe (ya data analyst ko) stats dikhao — ho sakta hai market regime badli ho, filter tweak karna pade (ye system self-upgrading hai kyunki pura backtest engine saath hai).
  4. Capital scaling rule: 3 mahine consistently positive (monthly avgR > +1.5R) → tabhi capital/risk badhao. Pehle process prove karo, phir size.
  5. Naye filters tabhi add karo jab wo same backtest engine pe test hue hon — "sunne me acha lag raha hai" koi filter nahi hota (Part 10 ka table yaad rakho).
